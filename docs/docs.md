    <h1 style="font-size: 32px; margin-bottom: 10px; color: var(--logo-color);">📖 OneUI Template Setup Guide</h1>
    <p style="color: var(--text-color); opacity: 0.8; margin-bottom: 30px;">Follow this step-by-step guide to successfully install and configure your OneUI Blogger Template.</p>

    <div class="post-content" style="background: var(--card-bg); padding: 30px; border-radius: 12px; border: 1px solid var(--border-color);">
        
        <!-- Step 1 -->
        <h2>Step 1: Backup Your Existing Blog</h2>
        <p>Before installing any new theme, it is always recommended to take a backup of your current Blogger layout and widgets so you don't lose any existing content.</p>
        <ol>
            <li>Go to your <a href="https://www.blogger.com" target="_blank">Blogger Dashboard</a>.</li>
            <li>Navigate to <strong>Theme</strong>.</li>
            <li>Click on the dropdown arrow next to the <strong>Customize</strong> button on the right side.</li>
            <li>Click <strong>Backup</strong> and download your current XML theme file to your computer.</li>
        </ol>

        <hr style="border: 0; border-top: 1px solid var(--border-color); margin: 30px 0;">

        <!-- Step 2 -->
        <h2>Step 2: Upload the OneUI XML Theme</h2>
        <p>Once you have downloaded your theme package (Free or Pro edition), extract the zip file to find the main <code>.xml</code> template file.</p>
        <ol>
            <li>In your Blogger Dashboard, go to <strong>Theme</strong>.</li>
            <li>Click the dropdown arrow next to <strong>Customize</strong> and select <strong>Restore</strong>.</li>
            <li>Click <strong>Upload</strong> and choose the OneUI XML file from your extracted folder.</li>
            <li>Wait a few seconds for the upload to complete. Once done, your site is now running on OneUI!</li>
        </ol>

        <hr style="border: 0; border-top: 1px solid var(--border-color); margin: 30px 0;">

        <!-- Step 3 -->
        <h2>Step 3: Enable Mobile Template Settings</h2>
        <p>To ensure the modern app-like layout displays properly on mobile devices:</p>
        <ol>
            <li>In the Blogger Dashboard, go to <strong>Theme</strong>.</li>
            <li>Click the gear icon (⚙️) under the Mobile Preview section.</li>
            <li>Select <strong>"No. Show desktop theme on mobile devices."</strong> and click Save.</li>
        </ol>

        <hr style="border: 0; border-top: 1px solid var(--border-color); margin: 30px 0;">

        <!-- Step 4 -->
        <h2>Step 4: Configuring Layout & Widgets</h2>
        <p>Go to the <strong>Layout</strong> section in Blogger to manage your sidebar widgets, header links, social icons, and advertisement banners according to your preference.</p>

        <div style="background: rgba(52, 152, 219, 0.1); border-left: 4px solid #3498db; padding: 15px; border-radius: 4px; margin-top: 20px;">

        </div>
