## 📱 What is OneUI Blogger Template?

The **OneUI Blogger Template** is a high-performance, minimalist, and UI-focused web template built for the Google Blogger (Blogspot) platform. Its design is inspired by modern mobile user interfaces (such as Samsung's OneUI or clean Material Design), delivering an exceptionally smooth and professional look across both desktop and mobile devices.

Whether you are publishing tech news, running a tutorial site, or managing a personal blog, OneUI presents your content in a structured and eye-catching format.
