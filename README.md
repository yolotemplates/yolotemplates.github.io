## 🛠️ How to Install a Theme

1. **Purchase & Download:** Complete your purchase and download the `.xml` theme file.
2. **Backup:** Go to your Blogger Dashboard > **Theme** > Click the down arrow > **Backup** to save your current theme.
3. **Restore/Upload:** Click the down arrow again > **Restore** > **Upload** and select your new YoloTemplates `.xml` file.
4. **Customize:** Navigate to the **Layout** section to set up your logo, menus, and widgets.
